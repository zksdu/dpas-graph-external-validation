## Appendix v1.3: Reporting standards for RNA-to-protein prediction (2026-09-14)

A transferable summary of the negative-control and baseline practices used in this study, proposed as a community reporting standard (corresponding to the "community reporting standard" paragraph in the Discussion):

1. **Fully external validation**: hold out external sections in their entirety (no other section from the same study may enter training), and report a sample-overlap test between training and external data (e.g. same-barcode correlations);
2. **Mandatory linear baseline**: every RNA-to-protein prediction model should report a ridge-on-PCA (or equivalent linear) competing baseline — the absence of such a baseline is precisely why "graph models work" conclusions have survived;
3. **Per-protein metrics**: report per-protein distributions (Spearman), not only spot-level Pearson (the latter is dominated by the shared mean-abundance profile; a ~0.78 spot-level correlation is not spatial fidelity);
4. **Isotype-control noise floor**: define the floor as the per-compartment p95 |rho| of isotype virtual proteins and report it alongside the model; at full training budget the floor can rise (tonsil fold: 0.178 floor vs 0.196 31-marker mean in this study);
5. **Seed replication and variance**: key conclusions should use at least three independent seeds, reported as mean ± SD (here 0.274 ± 0.029);
6. **No omnibus spatial test in isolation**: Kruskal–Wallis / eta-squared carry no explanatory power when isotype controls are equally significant (here controls 0.431/0.524 vs real 0.395/0.588).

## Appendix v1.4: DGAT matched-spot rerun (2026-09-14, RTX 6000D GPU)

Reviewer-risk item — the DGAT comparison being confounded by its built-in spot filtering — was quantified by a supplementary experiment. Mechanistic clarification: DGAT's default 700-gene filter operates *within* the 4,000-marker panel matrix (one lymph-node section retains only 16/3,024 spots), whereas this study's >=500-gene QC is applied on the full transcriptome (18,085 genes) before panel subsetting (`scripts/fix_prep_proc.py`, `qc_filter`), retaining all in-tissue spots of the four external sections. The like-for-like comparison therefore removes all DGAT-side spot filtering (both architectures evaluated on identical spot sets).

| Condition | 31-marker Spearman | Fold means |
|---|---|---|
| DGAT reference pipeline (700-of-panel) | 0.341 | 0.275 / 0.408 |
| DGAT panel-internal 500 (intermediate variant) | 0.306 | 0.272 / 0.340 |
| DGAT matched spot sets (no additional filtering, both folds retrained) | **0.240** | 0.227 / 0.253 |
| DPAS-Graph full budget (three seeds) | 0.274 ± 0.029 | — |

Under matched spot sets DGAT is on par with DPAS-Graph (0.240 vs 0.241) and beats ridge on only 4/31 markers — the "architectural advantage" seen under the reference pipeline is a spot-set artefact, strengthening the central negative conclusion.
